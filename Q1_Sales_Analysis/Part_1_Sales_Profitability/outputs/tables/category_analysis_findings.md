# Category Sales & Profitability Findings

## Overall Performance

The analysis shows clear differences between the three product categories across
sales, average profit per order containing that category, and profit margin.

### Electronics

Electronics generated the highest total sales at ₹165,267 and the highest
average profit per order containing Electronics at ₹51.44. However, its profit margin was 6.35%,
which was lower than Clothing's 8.03% margin.

The sub-category analysis shows that Printers contributed ₹5,964 in profit,
while Accessories generated a strong 16.38% profit margin. In contrast,
Electronic Games generated a loss of ₹1,236 with a -3.16% margin, reducing
the overall profitability of the Electronics category.

**Recommendation:** Maintain focus on high-performing sub-categories such as
Printers and Accessories while reviewing the pricing, discounting, or cost
structure of Electronic Games.

---

## Clothing

Clothing generated ₹139,054 in sales and ₹11,163 in total profit. Although
its sales were lower than Electronics, it generated the highest total profit
and the highest profit margin at 8.03%.

Several sub-categories contributed strong margins. T-shirts had a 20.32%
margin, followed by Shirts at 14.97%, Hankerchiefs at 14.36%, and Stoles at
13.80%.

However, Sarees generated ₹53,511 in sales but only ₹352 in profit, resulting
in a very low 0.66% margin.

**Recommendation:** Protect and expand high-margin clothing segments while
investigating the low profitability of Sarees through pricing, discount, and
cost analysis.

---

## Furniture

Furniture was the weakest-performing category across the three main metrics.
It generated ₹127,181 in sales, but only ₹2,298 in total profit. Its average
profit per order containing Furniture was ₹12.35 and its profit margin was only 1.81%.

The main concern is the Tables sub-category, which generated ₹22,614 in sales
but recorded a loss of ₹4,011, resulting in a -17.74% margin.

Bookcases performed considerably better, generating ₹4,888 in profit with an
8.60% margin.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the negative profitability of Tables and
prioritize profitable Furniture sub-categories such as Bookcases.

---

## Key Business Insight

Electronics was the strongest category by sales and average profit per order containing that category,
while Clothing was the strongest category by total profit and profit margin.

This demonstrates that higher sales do not necessarily translate into higher
profitability. Clothing generated ₹11,163 in total profit despite having
₹26,213 less sales than Electronics.

Furniture requires the greatest attention because it had the lowest average
profit per order containing that category and profit margin, with Tables being a significant
loss-making sub-category.
